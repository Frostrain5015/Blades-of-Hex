This is the sole star chronicle (将星列传) for the Blades of Hex campaign system, titled **染血的鸢尾花** (Bloodstained Iris). The former standalone chronicle **我心如火** has been merged into this one as Chapter 2, with its tutorial battlefield materials distributed across the four teaching flashback levels.

**Key facts:**
- Setting: Aurelia Kingdom (Greek-Roman style, no supernatural)
- Theme: 同一句誓约的两种读法 — politics, conspiracy, brotherhood, sacrifice
- 4 chapters × 15 levels (4 teaching + 7 normal + 4 boss)
- 6 characters: Marcus (centurion), Varo (ironGuard), Agrius (berserker), Cato (minister), Severus (advisor), King Adrian (NPC)

**Chapter structure:**
| Chapter | Name | Levels | Story |
|---------|------|--------|-------|
| 1 | 雨夜孤城 | T1-5 | Eastern expedition, siege of Petra, Cato's death. Marcus discovers first crack (edict fragment) |
| 2 | 我心如火 | 6-9 | King escapes, hunt in snow, Agrius standoff (non-lethal boss). Truth revealed in hunting lodge |
| 3 | 同一个誓言 | 10-13 | Defection, assembling forces, Varo's city gate battle. "We made the same oath." |
| 4 | 鸢尾落处 | 14-15 | Street fight, Severus throne room final boss, branching ending |

**Teaching levels (T1 / T3 / T7 / T12):** Flashbacks to Marcus training new recruits. Each unlocks a mechanic used in the subsequent normal level. The original 《我心如火》 rain-city map assets are distributed into T1 (basic operations) and T3 (terrain/type triangle) flashback maps.

**Boss sequence:** Cato (Petra Chapter 1) → Agrius (lodge Chapter 2, non-lethal) → Varo (city gate Chapter 3) → Severus (throne Chapter 4)

**Key relationships:**
- Marcus & Varo: former brothers-in-arms, same oath, opposite readings
- Agrius: sole surviving royal guard, concealed for 3 years, emerges in Chapter 2
- Cato: last loyal minister, his death is the first domino
- Severus: master of narrative, never fights until the final room
